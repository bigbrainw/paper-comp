PaperComp

Read papers on iPad, write on them with Apple Pencil, and box any passage to have it explained on your device.

  • Import PDFs, annotate with pen and highlighter, and add blank note pages or notebooks.
  • Box a term, equation, or figure to explain it, define it, or find the cited paper.
  • Answers come from a model that runs on your iPad. GPT is optional, with your own OpenAI key.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking.
